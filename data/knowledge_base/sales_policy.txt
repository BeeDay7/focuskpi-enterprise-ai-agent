SALES ANALYTICS POLICY

Document ID: SALES-001
Department: Revenue Operations
Version: 1.0

Purpose

This policy defines how sales information should be interpreted and reported.

Approved analytics

Regional sales totals may be used to identify high-performing and under-performing regions.

Sales totals must be calculated from approved transaction records.

Revenue figures should identify the relevant reporting period.

AI-generated summaries must not alter source transaction values.

Data interpretation

A high-sales region is a region with comparatively strong recorded revenue for the selected reporting period.

Sales performance does not by itself establish profitability.

The AI assistant must distinguish recorded sales from forecasts and predictions.

Reporting controls

Users requesting sensitive financial information must be authenticated and authorized before protected information is returned.

When data is incomplete, the assistant must disclose the limitation.

The assistant must not fabricate missing transactions.

Auditability

Analytics requests should be logged with timestamp, requesting identity, query intent, and tools used whenever audit logging is enabled.
