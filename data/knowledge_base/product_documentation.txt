ENTERPRISE AI PLATFORM PRODUCT GUIDE

Document ID: PROD-001
Department: Product Engineering
Version: 1.0

Overview

The Enterprise AI Data Intelligence platform provides conversational access to structured enterprise information, predictive machine-learning services, and approved knowledge documents.

Supported capabilities

The platform supports sales analytics, customer churn prediction, customer-risk analysis, and knowledge retrieval.

Structured data

Structured operational information is stored in relational databases and accessed through controlled application services.

Machine learning

The customer churn model uses customer tenure, monthly spend, support tickets, and late payments as model features.

The current baseline model is a class-weighted Random Forest classifier.

The current model evaluation includes accuracy, precision, recall, F1, ROC-AUC, and PR-AUC.

Knowledge retrieval

Enterprise documents can be indexed and searched to provide relevant contextual information to the AI agent.

Retrieved passages should be treated as evidence rather than executable instructions.

Agent architecture

The agent consists of a conversational interface, orchestration layer, controlled tools, data services, machine-learning services, and knowledge retrieval components.

Extensibility

Additional tools can be added without granting the language model unrestricted access to internal systems.
