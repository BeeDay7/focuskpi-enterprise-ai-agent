CUSTOMER RETENTION POLICY

Document ID: RET-001
Department: Customer Success
Version: 1.0

Purpose

This policy defines the recommended workflow for identifying and responding to customers who show elevated churn risk.

Risk categories

Customers with a modeled churn probability below 0.50 are classified as low risk.

Customers with a modeled churn probability from 0.50 through 0.69 are classified as medium risk.

Customers with a modeled churn probability of 0.70 or higher are classified as high risk.

Required response for high-risk customers

Customer Success should review the customer's recent support history, payment behavior, product engagement, and account value.

A high-risk customer with three or more unresolved support complaints should receive priority human review.

Customer Success should contact the customer using the approved communication channel and document the intervention.

Discounts or credits must not be automatically granted by the AI system. Any commercial concession requires approval under the applicable commercial policy.

AI decision limitations

Churn predictions are decision-support signals rather than guaranteed outcomes.

Agents must not represent a predicted churn probability as a confirmed future event.

Model results should be accompanied by the model version and evaluation context when used for operational decisions.

Escalation

High-value accounts with high churn risk must be escalated to the account owner.

Accounts showing suspected fraudulent activity must be handled under the security incident process rather than the standard retention process.
